Mission & Vision

Empowering Female Engineers to Lead with Voice & Vision

At Gouthami Institute of Technology and Management for Women (GITAMW), technical excellence meets articulate leadership. PDCC / PBCC is a student-led initiative designed to transform stage fear into confident expression and career readiness.

Speak with Authority

Master public speaking, impromptu addresses, and vocal presence. Say what you mean with clarity and poise.

Core Pillar 2

Connect & Network

Build meaningful cross-department relationships across 1st to 4th year CSE, ECE, EEE and all branches.

Core Pillar 3

Placement Edge

Conquer Group Discussions, Technical Defense, and HR interview rounds with authentic executive presence.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about joining and participating in the club.

Is there any fee to join the club?

No! Membership in PDCC / PBCC is 100% free for all students of Gouthami Institute of Technology and Management for Women.

Who is eligible to register?

Students from all years (1st, 2nd, 3rd, and 4th year) and all engineering branches (CSE, ECE, EEE, CIVIL, MECH, etc.) are welcome to join.

When and where do sessions take place?

Weekly sessions are held on campus during designated activity hours, planned carefully so they do not conflict with regular class timetables.

Will I receive a certificate for active participation?

Yes! Active club members receive official GITAMW participation and event winner certificates to highlight on resumes and LinkedIn profiles.

How do I register right now?

Simply tap the "Join the club" button on this portal, fill in your basic details (Name, Email, Phone, Branch), and your spot will be reserved instantly!
